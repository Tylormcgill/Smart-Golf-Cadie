HOW THIS WORKS

1. Search the exact course.
2. Select it.
3. Start GPS.
4. The app finds the nearest mapped green and calculates your distance to it.

GPS accuracy varies by phone, sky conditions and course mapping. The app shows the reported GPS accuracy instead of pretending the yardage is exact.
